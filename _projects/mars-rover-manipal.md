From November 2022 to September 2025, I worked across the rover as a complete mechanical system and ultimately served as **Mechanical Design & Manufacturing Head**. My scope covered architecture, detailed design, fabrication, assembly, iteration, and competition deployment—not only one subsystem.

## Rover systems

- A **4-bar robotic arm** for manipulation tasks.
- **Cycloidal drives** developed for compact, high-reduction actuation.
- A modular chassis designed around integration and serviceability.
- Custom **3D-printed wheels** and an optimized **5-bar suspension**.
- Manufacturing planning, assembly, and integration across core mechanical subsystems.

## In-situ science module

I developed a 3D-printed soil collection and analysis module built around a dual-auger leadscrew mechanism. It drills to a depth of **10 cm**, integrates **six sensors**, and includes sample caching and fluid delivery for soil analysis.

Moving from one auger to two and optimizing the operating cycle enabled up to **100% faster sampling and sensor cycles**. The system is covered by patent application **202541099166**.

## Competition results

- Back-to-back **International Rover Challenge wins** in 2024 and 2025.
- **Top 10** at the European Rover Challenge 2025.
- **18th overall and 3rd in the Science Mission** at the University Rover Challenge 2024.

## Leadership and supporting software

I led mechanical design and manufacturing while also directing technical recruitment across more than 1,000 applicants. A PyGame-based rover interface was a smaller supporting contribution within the larger rover program; its [source is available on GitHub](https://github.com/VipulDinesh/MRM-Pygame-GUI).
